# Lessons learned

Distilled, actionable knowledge promoted from projects or debugging sessions.

## What belongs here

- "When X, do Y" knowledge.
- Patterns that prevented or caused problems.
- Decisions whose outcome taught us something.

## What does NOT belong here

- Raw debugging stories → `../debugging/`
- Evergreen principles → `../permanent/`
- Project-specific trivia → `../projects/`
