# Technology notes

One note per technology, library, language, framework, tool, or platform.

## What belongs here

- What the technology is in our terms and why we use it.
- Gotchas, limitations, version-specific behaviors we've verified.
- Patterns we use with it.
- Alternatives we evaluated.

## What does NOT belong here

- A project's use of the technology → `../projects/`
- A general engineering concept → `../permanent/`
- A how-to that should be reusable as a skill → `../../skills/`

## Naming

`<technology>.md`, kebab-case. Prefer a single canonical note per technology; split only when the surface area is genuinely large.
