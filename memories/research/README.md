# Research notes

Time-stamped exploration before conclusions are stable.

## What belongs here

- Spikes, evaluations, and "what if" investigations.
- Comparisons of options with sources and confidence levels.
- Raw findings before they are distilled into permanent knowledge.

## What does NOT belong here

- Stable conclusions → promote to `../permanent/`, `../technologies/`, or `../lessons/`
- Project-specific constraints → `../projects/`
- Tool how-tos → `../../skills/`

## Lifecycle

A research note starts `active`. When its findings stabilize, promote the knowledge to the right permanent note, link back, and archive the research note.

Use [`../../templates/research-note.md`](../../templates/research-note.md).
